Privacy policy

Last updated 3 October 2026

Oh My Dare is a dare game for couples. It has no accounts and no sign-in. This page says exactly what the app collects, what it never collects, and how to turn collection off.

What stays on your device

  • The two names you enter.
  • Custom dares you write.
  • Your play history: which dares you have seen, your streak, and your purchase status.

None of this is sent anywhere. "Reset progress" in Settings erases it.

What we never see

Your names, your email, and your custom dares. The app does not ask for an email, does not read the accounts on your phone, and never transmits anything you type.

Anonymous play stats

To learn which dares work and which get skipped, the app sends anonymous usage events to our analytics provider, PostHog (servers in the United States). Each event may include:

  • a random identifier created by the app on first launch, not linked to you or any account;
  • the mood, game and pack chosen, the dare shown (its id and text for built-in dares only), and whether it was done or skipped;
  • a play-session identifier, the app version, and whether the full set of dares is unlocked;
  • if you choose to rate an evening or send feedback from Settings, the star rating and the text you typed. This is sent anonymously and only when you tap Send;
  • technical details the analytics library records automatically: device model, operating system version, screen size, and language. Your IP address is discarded on receipt and is not used to work out your location.

Events are queued on the device and sent when the internet is available. Playing offline works fully.

Who receives data

  • PostHog, our analytics provider, receives the anonymous play stats described above and processes them on our behalf.
  • Google Play handles the one-time purchase and shares only the purchase status with the app, never your payment details.

Nobody else receives anything. We do not sell data, do not use advertising networks, and do not share data with other companies for their own purposes.

Security

Everything the app sends travels over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). Everything you type stays on your device.

Turning it off

Settings has a switch, Share anonymous play stats. Turn it off and nothing further is collected or sent. The switch is on by default and your choice is kept, including after Reset progress.

Purchases

The one-time unlock is sold through Google Play. Google handles payment; we never see your payment details. Google's own privacy policy applies to that transaction. Purchases are tied to your Google account by Google Play, which is how "restore" works on another device.

Permissions

The app requests only internet access, used solely for the anonymous stats above and for the Google Play purchase.

Children

Oh My Dare is for adults, 18 and over. It is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect anything from them.

Retention and deletion

Anonymous events are kept by our analytics provider to study how the game is played. Because they carry no name, email or account, we cannot link them to you on request; turning the switch off stops any further collection, and uninstalling the app removes the random identifier so nothing new can be associated with it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it, and material changes will be noted in the app.
